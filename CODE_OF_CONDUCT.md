# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want a technical community where people can disagree about implementations without attacking one another. Participation is open regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, constructive and respectful.
- Focus criticism on code, contracts and evidence.
- Welcome new contributors and explain project boundaries clearly.
- Respect privacy and never post credentials, customer data or private employee packages.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and repair them where possible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, publishing private information, and retaliation against people reporting a problem are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

These rules apply in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and official project spaces. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, suspend participation or escalate serious abuse.

Report conduct concerns privately to **arazerx@gmail.com**. Do not include secrets or unnecessary personal data. Maintainers will review reports in good faith while protecting the reporter's privacy. No fixed response-time guarantee is made.
